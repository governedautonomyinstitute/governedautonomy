# Governed Autonomy Framework

Six Pillars that organize design and operation.

The six Pillars organize design and operation: Agent Identity; Mission Definition; Behavioral Policy; Runtime Enforcement; Human Oversight and Intervention; Multi-Agent Governance. They correspond to the five [Architectural Planes](https://governedautonomy.org/architecture/) described in the [v4 text](https://governedautonomy.org/doctrine/). They identify responsibilities rather than a complete product specification. Implementation interfaces, organizational staffing and operating procedures belong in deployment profiles and adopter guidance.

Identity and mission are separate concerns within Plane 1, which is why five Planes correspond to six Pillars. The Pillars identify what must be designed and operated; implementation contracts and assessment evidence specify how a particular deployment demonstrates it.
