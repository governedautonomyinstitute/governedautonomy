# Proposal Log

Every proposed change, its status and its disposition, in public.

Feedback is accepted at any time at [rfc@governedautonomy.org](mailto:rfc@governedautonomy.org). Each proposed change receives a stable identifier and appears here on the day it is received, with a non-identifying summary. The Institute reviews it and records one disposition and the reason. The Executive Director releases accepted changes as minor versions, unless the change is major under the rule in [About](https://governedautonomy.org/about/#r25). This log records changes to the doctrine. Presentation, formatting, links and citations are corrected directly and are not logged. A received entry is not adopted doctrine. A private attachment may protect sensitive evidence but never replaces the visible issue, review and disposition. See [how changes are accepted and released](https://governedautonomy.org/about/#how-changes-are-accepted-and-released).
